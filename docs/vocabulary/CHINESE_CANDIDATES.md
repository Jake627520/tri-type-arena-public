# Chinese candidate vocabulary

The seed list in `scripts/data/chinese/seed.txt` was independently proposed for daily-life typing practice, without reading the previous vocabulary or frequency lists. It is not a frequency ranking. The first 300 structurally eligible rows in `candidates.tsv` form both candidate pools. The tier assignments preserve application quotas and do not establish linguistic difficulty. New IDs avoid mapping old adaptive progress to different words.

Candidates are selected independently by daily-life theme and matched only to CC-CEDICT (2026-10-31; mirror commit `55a0cf1226b044c59579dd8e0a32114ca6a14a11`; SHA-256 `b4537f6bdd73fa33f94c07ceda3ce697da66bf32826167cce3b7ba42c0f3755e`). A word is retained when it has one distinct CC-CEDICT reading with supported syllables. `node scripts/match-chinese-sources.mjs CEDICT --check` reconstructs the candidate table; `python3 scripts/generate-chinese.py --check` verifies the generated application data.

Zhuyin is a mechanical transcription of CC-CEDICT pinyin by [Austin Shelby's MIT-licensed converter](https://github.com/AustinShelby/pinyin-to-bopomofo/tree/1bed04959de65d8b793ea18aeb9fcaab20b7b4ff), bundled from that commit. The license is in `scripts/data/chinese/pinyin-to-bopomofo-LICENSE.txt` and in the public notices. This is not independently verified Taiwanese usage; regional pronunciation differences and neutral tones require review. McBopomofo and libtabe were used in an earlier candidate version, but neither determines selection or readings in the current table. Previous data still exist in repository history.

Automated uniqueness and formatting checks do not prove the chosen reading is appropriate to the intended context or locale. Human linguistic review has not been completed.
