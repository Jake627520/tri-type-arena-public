# English vocabulary source: Moby Words II

The current 500 English entries are generated from Grady Ward's **Moby Words II** frequency list. The author's documentation states that the material was placed in the public domain by the author in January 2001: [documentation](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/3201/3201-h/3201-h.htm), [source list](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/3201/files/freq.txt), [Project Gutenberg catalogue](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/3201). Gutenberg describes the material as public domain in the USA; treatment in other jurisdictions and any remaining upstream rights require review. This record is evidence of the publisher's statements, not legal clearance or a guarantee.

The locally retained source `scripts/data/moby-freq.txt` was downloaded from the source URL on 2026-11-02; its SHA-256 is `ace4ecc06acf0d3df397cf4d680d0d9c7a308e4860c10eb9b9edf2f4e31076c9`. The dated source is historical and must not be described as a current frequency ranking. Project Gutenberg's name is not used as a trademark endorsement of this adaptation.

Run `node scripts/generate-moby-english.mjs` to reproduce the generated file. The script verifies the hash, trims surrounding whitespace, excludes non-ASCII-letter entries, lowercases and deduplicates in source order, then selects the first 200 words of 2–4 letters, 200 of 5–6 letters, and 100 of 7 or more letters. These tiers indicate typing length, not linguistic proficiency. The transformation changes the list and preserves neither its complete contents nor its original ranking across tiers.

New `en-moby-*` IDs prevent older adaptive progress from being associated with different words. Previous English lesson progress remains stored but is not carried over to the new entries. The original English data remains in Git history and requires separate public-release review. The owner accepts publication based on the documented author statement; no independent legal clearance or complete linguistic review is asserted.
